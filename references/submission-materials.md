# Submission-material format specification

These files use layouts distinct from the main manuscript.

## Highlights

### Page

- A4 portrait.
- Top margin: 2.54 cm.
- Bottom margin: 2.54 cm.
- Left margin: 3.175 cm.
- Right margin: 3.175 cm.

### Title

- Text may be `Highlights` or a user-supplied equivalent.
- Arial, 18 pt, bold.
- Justified/left visual placement as in the source file.
- Line spacing: 2.0.

### Bullet items

- Arial, 14 pt.
- Justified.
- Line spacing: 2.0.
- Bullet list.
- Hanging indent: 0.741 cm.
- Preserve the user's bullet content exactly unless content editing is separately requested.

## Conflict of Interest

### Page

- A4 portrait.
- Top/bottom margins: 2.54 cm.
- Left/right margins: 3.175 cm.

### Title

- Arial, 18 pt, bold.
- Line spacing: 2.0.

### Body

- Arial, 14 pt.
- Justified.
- First-line indent: 0.741 cm.
- Line spacing: 2.0.

Do not supply a conflict declaration unless the user provides or requests the wording. This skill defines formatting, not legal substance.

## Supplementary Caption

### Page

- A4 portrait.
- Top/bottom margins: 2.54 cm.
- Left/right margins: 3.175 cm.

### Title

- Arial, 18 pt, bold.
- Line spacing: 2.0.

### Caption body

- Arial, 14 pt.
- Justified.
- First-line indent: 0.741 cm.
- Line spacing: 2.0.
- Bold the media label only, for example `Supplementary Video.`; keep descriptive text regular.

## Cover Letter

### Page

- A4 portrait.
- Top margin: 2.54 cm.
- Bottom margin: 2.54 cm.
- Left margin: 1.895 cm.
- Right margin: 1.895 cm.

### Body typography

- Times New Roman, 12 pt.
- Line spacing: 1.25.
- Paragraph space before: 7.8 pt for normal letter paragraphs.
- Body first-line indent: 0.847 cm.
- Body alignment: justified.

### Special paragraphs

- Date: 12 pt, right aligned, no first-line indent.
- Salutation such as `Dear Editors:`: 12 pt, no first-line indent.
- Closing such as `Yours sincerely,`: 12 pt, no first-line indent.
- Signature/name/affiliation/address lines: 12 pt, no first-line indent.
- Preserve any institutional letterhead/logo region if present in the user's source.

This file's formatting rules do not authorize generation of submission claims, originality statements, or declarations unless the user asks for content drafting.
